Most pilgrims arrive in Tirupati by air or by train, tired from the journey and keen to get to their hotel or straight up to Tirumala. Knowing your options in advance saves a lot of confusion at the arrival gate, especially if you land or pull in late at night.

This guide looks at the three main arrival points: Tirupati Airport, Tirupati railway station and Renigunta Junction.

## Tirupati Airport

Tirupati Airport is at Renigunta, outside the city. It is roughly 15 km from central Tirupati.

### Getting into Tirupati

- **Pre-booked taxi:** the simplest option. Your driver can meet you at arrivals and take you straight to your hotel.
- **Taxis and cabs at the airport:** usually available, though availability can be patchy for late flights.
- **Buses:** there are bus services connecting the airport area with the city, but frequency varies, so check locally.

The drive into the city typically takes around 30 to 45 minutes, depending on traffic.

### Going straight to Tirumala

If you want to go directly up the hill, allow roughly an hour and a half or more. The route takes you through Tirupati to Alipiri, where vehicles and luggage are checked, and then up the ghat road. Traffic at the checkpoint and on the ghat can add time, particularly on weekends and festival days.

## Tirupati railway station

Tirupati's main railway station sits right in the centre of the city, close to the main bus stand and the Govindaraja Swamy Temple. This makes it the most convenient arrival point for many pilgrims.

### Getting into the city

Most hotels in Tirupati are a short ride from the station. Autorickshaws and taxis are available outside, and if you have booked a car, your driver can meet you at the station entrance.

### Going up to Tirumala

Alipiri, at the foot of the hills, is only a few kilometres from the station. From there it is the ghat road up to Tirumala. In all, the journey from the station to Tirumala usually takes somewhere around an hour, though it can be longer on busy days.

TTD buses also run from the city up to Tirumala. Check the current arrangements locally or on TTD's official website at [ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in](https://ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in).

## Renigunta Junction

Renigunta Junction is a major railway junction about 10 km from Tirupati, and several long-distance trains stop here rather than at Tirupati itself. It is also close to the airport.

### Getting into Tirupati and Tirumala

- The drive from Renigunta to central Tirupati is roughly 20 to 30 minutes.
- To Tirumala, allow roughly an hour and a quarter to an hour and a half, depending on checks and traffic.
- Taxis and autorickshaws are usually available, but there are fewer options than at Tirupati station, particularly at night.

If your train arrives at Renigunta, a pre-booked pick-up makes a big difference.

## Tips for late trains and flights

Trains run late, flights get delayed, and arriving at midnight with luggage and family in tow is not much fun if you have no plan. A few suggestions:

- **Book your pick-up in advance.** Share your train number or flight number so your driver can track delays.
- **Keep your phone charged.** Save your driver's number and your hotel's number before you travel.
- **Confirm your hotel check-in.** Let your hotel know you will arrive late so your room is held.
- **Think twice about going straight up the hill at night.** Ghat access rules and timings can change, and security checks still apply. Check the current rules before planning a late-night drive to Tirumala.
- **Rest before darshan.** If you have an early darshan slot, it is often better to stay in Tirupati or reach Tirumala the evening before, rather than travelling through the night.
- **Check what you are carrying.** Vehicles and bags are inspected at Alipiri. Items that are not allowed on the hill, such as alcohol, tobacco and non-vegetarian food, are best left behind.

## Planning the return journey

On your way back, give yourself a generous margin. The descent from Tirumala, plus city traffic and any delays, can easily add up. As a rough guide, many travellers aim to leave Tirumala at least three hours before a flight and a couple of hours before a train, and more on busy days.

Remember too that phones are not allowed inside the main temple. If you are depositing your phone before darshan, keep your driver's number written down somewhere.
