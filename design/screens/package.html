Overview

Visit the most important temples in and around Tirupati in one comfortable day. Your driver plans the route around darshan timings and crowds, so you spend your time in the temples rather than in traffic.

8–9 hoursStarts 6–7 AM
5 templesRoute optimised
1–20 peopleSedan to mini bus
Door pickupHotel / station

Itinerary

  1. 1

    Sri Padmavathi Ammavari Temple, Tiruchanur

    Start early for a shorter queue. About 1.5 hours.

  2. 2

    Sri Govindaraja Swamy Temple

    In the heart of Tirupati town. About 1 hour.

  3. 3

    Sri Kapileswara Swamy Temple

    Shiva temple at the foot of the hills, with a waterfall. About 45 minutes.

  4. 4

    Sri Kalyana Venkateswara Swamy Temple

    Srinivasa Mangapuram. About 1 hour.

  5. 5

    ISKCON Tirupati & drop-off

    Evening visit, then drop at your hotel or station.

  • Fuel and driver allowance
  • Pickup and drop within Tirupati
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Darshan or entry tickets
